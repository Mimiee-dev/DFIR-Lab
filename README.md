response and continuous learning.

## 🧪 Investigation methodology

My investigations will document the following stages:

1. **Identification** — define the incident and evidence sources.
2. **Preservation** — protect evidence and document its integrity.
3. **Collection** — acquire authorised evidence using appropriate methods.
4. **Examination** — extract relevant artefacts and metadata.
5. **Analysis** — correlate evidence and construct a timeline.
6. **Reporting** — document findings, limitations and recommendations.

## ⚠️ Ethics and disclaimer

All investigations are intended for authorised, controlled learning
environments. No unauthorised systems or personal data should be
examined. Sample evidence should be synthetic, public training data
or otherwise lawfully obtained.

Findings will be reported accurately, with limitations clearly stated.
This educational portfolio is not a claim of professional forensic
certification or law-enforcement expertise.
